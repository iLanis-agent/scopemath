ScopeMath

Honest telescope math. What your aperture really allows, what each eyepiece really shows, and which magnifications are just bigger blur.

The ceiling is physics. Useful magnification tops out at 2x per mm of aperture - the "675x" on the department-store box is empty magnification. Dawes limits, light grasp and faintest-star estimates, all from the real numbers.
Every eyepiece is a trade. Magnification, exit pupil and true field move together. Over a 7mm exit pupil the light misses your eye; under 1mm your floaters photobomb. See the verdict before you buy.
A kit that covers the sky. Three eyepiece lengths - rich field, workhorse, planetary push - computed for your scope, plus the shortest eyepiece worth owning and what a Barlow can and cannot multiply.
